# Type 3 universality

For every rank-two lattice generated by \(a,b\in\mathbb C\), Lean gives a
homeomorphism from the published Type 3 face complex to \(\mathbb C/(\mathbb Za+
\mathbb Zb)\) and a closed embedding into \(\mathbb R^3\). On each labeled face,
the homeomorphism is projection of an affine planar lift and the spatial
embedding preserves all distances in that lift. This is the universality
statement of Doyle, Lander, Schwartz, and Trettel.
[Lean theorem](../../OrigamiTorus/EightVertex/Type3/Universal.lean) · [Theorem 1.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.21909v1#S1.SS1)

The construction uses the paper's two planar families on
\(0\le x\le 1/6\) and \(1/6\le x\le 1/2\), with \(y>0\) and
\(y^2\ge 1-x^2\). The shared coordinates store the second coordinate in
units scaled by \(y\). The edge table retains diagonal \(12\), and the face
lifts include \(012\) and \(152\) with zero period shifts. Lean checks the
edge lengths, opposite side pairings, vertex links, positive face areas and
total area of one lattice cell.
[Edge data](../../OrigamiTorus/EightVertex/Type3/Data.lean) · [Periodic faces](../../OrigamiTorus/EightVertex/Type3/Periodic.lean)

Lean checks the 70 determinant signs and the 138 disjoint edge–face tests
for the paper's height vectors over both closed charts. It uses exact strip
polynomials in \(y^2-(1-x^2)\) and complementary horizontal coordinates.
When the heights are scaled by a small positive number, the implicit
function theorem corrects the horizontal coordinates of the folded and
intrinsic sheets at fixed periods. Strict separation and positive
intrinsic area survive the correction.
[Sign checks](../../OrigamiTorus/EightVertex/Type6/Signs.lean) · [Inflation](../../OrigamiTorus/EightVertex/Type6/Inflation.lean)

The fixed-period length system implements the geometric inflation in
Remark 5.4. Lean uses an affine dependence of the four planar vertices to
transfer the already checked fixed-period Jacobian from diagonal \(05\) to
diagonal \(12\). This replaces the paper's calculation of the cone-angle
Jacobian in Lemma 5.3. For the planar quotient, positive face orientations
make the development open, and total area proves multiplicity one. This
gives the global quotient conclusion directly from the lift data.
