# Type 5 embeddings from the square up to the hexagon

For every \(0\le x<1/2\), Type 5 embeds the flat torus generated by \(A=(1,0)\) and \(B=(x,\sqrt{1-x^2})\) facewise isometrically in \(\mathbb R^3\). Equivalently, this covers every torus whose lattice has two equal-length generators meeting at an angle \(60^\circ<\theta\le90^\circ\), at any scale. The modulus of the hexagonal lattice is a limit point of this family. Existence there remains open.

There is also an explicit two-dimensional region approaching that endpoint. Write \(\delta=1/2-x\), \(B=(x,y)\), \(y>0\), and \(T=x^2+y^2\). The same conclusion holds throughout
\[
0<\delta\le 2^{-24},\qquad 1\le T\le 1+2^{-16}\delta .
\]
The proof below uses rigorous arithmetic in Python. The face list, oriented shifts and general squared-distance construction are available in Lean. [Verifier](../../scripts/type5_rectangles/rhombic.py) · [Lean reconstruction](../../OrigamiTorus/EightVertex/Type5/RadicalChart.lean)

## Choosing the core

Use the square marking, write \(p_1=0\), and put the three spatial anchors \(1,2,5\) at \((p_i,0)\). In the table, \((W,S,E,C,D,K,X,H)\) are rational constants. Set
\[
w=\delta-W\delta^2,\qquad q=w-\tfrac12,\qquad
d=D\delta,\qquad b=\frac{-q-K\delta^5}{1-2d/T}.
\]
The dot products with \(A,B\) specify the following intrinsic points.

| Vertex | \(p_i\cdot A\) | \(p_i\cdot B\) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | \(w-S\delta^3\) | \(-T/2+E\delta^{10}\) |
| 3 | \(1/2-C\delta^9\) | \(b\) |
| 5 | \(q\) | \(-T/2+d\) |

To choose \(p_4\), first impose height zero at an auxiliary position of leaf 0. Let \(M\) be reflection in the line through \(0,p_2\). Let \(P_j(u)\) denote the spatial horizontal coordinates determined by the equations to anchors 2 and 5 when \(p_j=u\). This is affine in \(u\). Put
\[
e=Mp_5-(p_5+A),\qquad f=MP_3(p_3)-p_3,\qquad
u=\frac{-w}{\det(e,f)}(f_y,-f_x).
\]
An intrinsic leaf at \(u\) and spatial leaf at \((Mu,0)\) already have the correct distances to 1 and 2. Subtracting the squared-distance equation to 1 from the equations to 5 and 3 gives \(e\cdot u=-w\) and \(f\cdot u=0\), respectively. These are exactly the two equations just solved.

The shift from 4 to 0 is \(L=B-A\). For a trial core point \(v\) at vertex 4, the remaining squared-distance equation for the auxiliary leaf is \(g(v)=0\), where
\[
g(v)=P_4(v)\cdot Mu-u\cdot(v-L)-v\cdot L+\lVert L\rVert^2/2.
\]
This follows by subtracting the norm equation at the origin and using \(\lVert r_4\rVert^2=\lVert v\rVert^2\). Write \(g(v)=g_0+g_xv_x+g_yv_y\). Use the actual core
\[
p_4=\left(X,-(g_0+g_xX)/g_y+H\delta^4/y\right).
\]
The checker verifies the divisions. With this perturbed core, reconstruct 3 and 4 on the positive and negative height branches, then solve afresh for leaves 0, 6 and 7 on branches \(+,-,+\). This step supplies the actual leaf coordinates on every cell. [Squared-distance equations](Type5.md#the-coordinates)

| Range of \(\delta\) | \(W\) | \(S\) | \(E\) | \(C\) | \(D\) | \(K\) | \(X\) | \(H\) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| \((0,2^{-24}]\), entire stated sector | 1/4 | 3/2 | 1/16 | 1/16 | 1/2 | 1/16 | −1/8 | 1/16 |
| \([2^{-24},7/16]\), \(T=1\) | 7/32 | 1/8 | 1/256 | 1/256 | 19/32 | 1/64 | −5/32 | 1/64 |

## Checking the parameter ranges

In the sector, put \(\delta=t^2\) and \(T=1+mt^2\), where \(0<t\le 1/4096\) and \(0\le m\le 1/65536\). Use Laurent remainder arithmetic in \(t\) with coefficients in successive real algebraic extensions of \(\mathbb Q(m)\). The five radicands in reconstruction order have leading powers \(12,4,8,26,30\) in \(t\). Factoring out those powers gives strictly positive enclosures over the sector. In particular the root heights go to zero at the excluded endpoint. [Laurent remainder proof](Type5Rectangles.md#checking-the-inequalities)

On the remainder of the arc the checker covers \([2^{-24},1/2]\) in 294 closed intervals. Write the parameter in each interval as an affine function of \(z\in[-1,1]\) and each intermediate value as \(P(z)+z^n R(z)\), where \(P\) is the Taylor polynomial of degree less than \(n\). Arb balls enclose all polynomial coefficients and \(R\). For multiplication, the terms of the polynomial product with degrees at least \(n\) contribute their shifted polynomial to the remainder. For division by \(g\), compute the truncated quotient \(Q\) and bound \((f-Qg)/(z^ng)\) by the high terms and input remainders, checking that \(g\) excludes zero throughout the interval. For a square root, compute the truncated root \(Q\) and use \((f-Q^2)/(z^n(\sqrt f+Q))\), checking both positivity conditions. Cancellation of the first \(n\) formal coefficients in each remainder is exact. [Arb Taylor arithmetic](../../scripts/type5_rectangles/ball_taylor.py)

On every cell the checker verifies positive area for all 16 marked faces and the alternating-minor separation test for all 138 disjoint edge–face pairs. The squared-length equations give congruent triangles. The signed area identity gives a degree-one intrinsic development, and the separation checks give spatial injectivity. Together these yield the claimed embedding. Each certified point also has an open neighborhood of realizable moduli by continuity of the chosen branch and its finitely many strict inequalities. [Geometric proof](Type5Rectangles.md#from-signs-to-a-torus)
