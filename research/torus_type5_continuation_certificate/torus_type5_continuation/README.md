# Nonrectangular Type 5 witness and hexagonal intrinsic feasibility

Run `python verify.py` and `python verify_hex_intrinsic.py` with Python 3 (standard library only). The recorded outputs are in the two verification text files. Do not run Python with assertions disabled.

## Type 5 witness

The certified modulus box has center (0.174241221618834, 1.084897889755590) and sup-norm radius 10^-11. The rational center and fixed intrinsic core coordinates were selected from numerical continuation, but they are exact inputs, not approximate solutions of equations. The root and leaf formulas construct solutions by radicals; integer interval arithmetic with denominator 2^200 certifies the signs uniformly on the whole box. The radius is conservative: direct interval evaluation at radius 10^-10 fails to certify; that failure is not an obstruction to existence.

The oriented face list and cocycle appear in verify.py. Vertex 1 is the origin, vertices 1,2,5 form the anchor triangle, vertices 3,4 are roots and vertices 0,6,7 are leaves. The graph is the join of K3 and K2,3. The anchor triangle has all shifts zero. In physical planar coordinates write s_i=p_i-K_ij for a neighbor i of a new vertex j. With intrinsic coordinate u and extrinsic coordinate v, the equations are |v-r_i|^2=|u-s_i|^2. Subtracting the equation for anchor 1 gives 2r_i dot v-2s_i dot u=|r_i|^2-|s_i|^2, while the anchor 1 equation is |v|^2=|u|^2. For a root, u is fixed; the anchor equations solve the two planar coordinates of v and its squared height. For a leaf, first solve the anchor equations for the planar part of v as an affine function of u. Eliminate the height from the two root equations, yielding a line in the u-plane. Parametrize that line, recover the height, and solve the remaining anchor 1 equation by the quadratic formula. The signs and denominators are checked by the verifier. Thus every edge equation holds identically for the radical expressions.

The cocycle closes on each face; each intrinsic triangle has the same nonzero orientation and total signed double area is between one and three times the covolume, hence its absolute degree is one. The intrinsic PL map to the flat torus is locally open at faces, edges and vertices: at a vertex the oriented nondegenerate triangles wind positively at least once. Absolute degree one therefore yields a homeomorphism. All four-point affine determinants are nonzero and all 138 disjoint edge-face intersections are excluded by the five-point affine circuit signs. General position ensures that any unintended simplex intersection would contain a minimal affine dependence on five points, necessarily a disjoint edge-face crossing. This proves embeddedness. The edge equalities give a facewise isometry and thus an isometry for intrinsic path metrics. No numerical root-solving accuracy is required.

## Intrinsic hexagonal feasibility

The prior winding reduction lists seven sharp-ray representatives for types 4,5,7. This script does not reprove the exhaustiveness of that list. For each listed cocycle K, in lattice coordinates solve the graph Laplacian equations sum_j(p_j-p_i+K_ij)=0 with p_0=0. Gaussian elimination over fractions gives rational intrinsic coordinates. At the hexagonal lattice the metric Gram matrix is [[1,1/2],[1/2,1]], and its Voronoi cell is determined by the three shortest directions (1,0),(0,1),(-1,1) and their negatives. The verifier checks strict closest-image inequalities, face orientation, face-shift consistency and absolute total signed double area 2 in lattice coordinates. By the same degree and openness argument this yields an intrinsic triangulation with each edge uniquely shortest. It does not yield an extrinsic realization. In particular, the shortest-edge constraints and intrinsic triangulation alone cannot exclude any of the four hexagonal marking orbits represented in the earlier reduction.

## Limitations

No universality or nonuniversality case is closed by this package. The new Type 5 box is small and does not cover the gap to the sharp-rhombic ray. Further numerical continuation reached x approximately 0.20726 but was close to degeneracy and is not certified. The earlier reductions, Type 2 nonexistence and Type 6/near-universality proofs have not been independently audited here. The hexagonal feasibility statement concerns only the intrinsic part of the systems, not the full three-dimensional problem. No novelty claim is made.
