## Exact six-vertex witness: obstruction cannot be confined to the tested subproblem

The shared signs force the affine Radon partition {1,3,7}|{2,5} on the complete five-vertex graph. This motivated the exact Gram kernel relaxation in `k5_kernel.py`. Both variants of its SMT formulation timed out in the attempted runs. A numerical search instead found a feasible partial configuration, now certified exactly on V={1,2,3,4,5,7} (14 edges and six inherited triangular faces).

Use coordinates (x,y) with scalar product x*x'+3*y*y'; the hexagonal lattice is generated by (1,0) and (1/2,1/2). Gauge vertex 5 to the intrinsic origin, with all incident edge shifts zero. With vertex 5 also the extrinsic origin, take r1,r2,r7 as the extrinsic basis. Its Gram matrix H has entries

    H_ij = <q_i,q_j> - <q_j-q_i,k_ij> - ||k_ij||^2/2

for i!=j and diagonal ||q_i||^2. Here k_ij is the lattice edge shift in this gauge. Positive definiteness of H is checked exactly by Sylvester's criterion. All entries are rational. For root i=3,4 write

    b_i(q)_a = <q_a,q> - <q-q_a,k_ai> - ||k_ai||^2/2, a=1,2,7.
    w_i(q) = H^{-1} b_i(q).
    b_i(q)^T H^{-1} b_i(q) = ||q||^2.

Each root thus satisfies a quadratic equation in its two intrinsic coordinates. For fixed rational x, the y coordinate is a quadratic radical; the root choices are checked rather than approximated. The extrinsic vector r_i is the combination of the three basis vectors with coefficients w_i(q). All edge equations hold exactly: the radial equality and the prescribed inner products recover the shifted squared edge lengths. No edge joins roots 3 and 4. For each root, constraints on nearest images, inherited triangle areas and four-point signs involving that root alone are affine in q for fixed H. Constraints involving both roots couple the conics. Choosing the extrinsic basis with negative ambient orientation gives the same normalized determinant convention as the branch ledger.

`verify_six.py` fixes the anchor and root x-coordinates to explicit rationals. It constructs the remaining two coordinates using the quadratic formula and verifies the strict inequalities with outward-rounded integer intervals of denominator 2^200. In particular:

- All 14 inherited edges are nonzero and uniquely shortest intrinsic lifts: the six nearest-lattice-neighbor halfspaces describe the hexagonal Voronoi cell.
- The six inherited triangles have the required negative double signed areas.
- All fifteen affine determinants on V have the strict common signs. Thus all smaller subsets are affinely independent. For a pair of simplices, any unwanted intersection gives a conformal affine circuit after cancelling shared coefficients. A minimal such circuit on V must have five vertices (general position); the only possible split between supports of dimensions at most two is 2+3. All such edge/face splits are explicitly checked, so the extrinsic partial subcomplex is embedded.
- The only missing pair 34 satisfies strict contraction to the intrinsic torus metric. We check all 25 translates with both integer coefficients between -2 and 2, inclusive. Any other lattice shift has norm at least sqrt(27/4), since a^2+ab+b^2 >= 3 max(a^2,b^2)/4. Each root is within distance 1/sqrt(3) of the origin, so any omitted translate has separation at least sqrt(27/4)-2/sqrt(3)>1/sqrt(3), while the extrinsic distance is strictly below 1/sqrt(3).

This yields a rigorous realization of the **tested six-vertex necessary subsystem**, not a full triangulation, nor a proof of any full orientation chamber's feasibility. No global intrinsic covering claim is made for this partial subcomplex. In particular the witness shows the K5 Radon circuit, both root conics, contraction of 34, and the inherited extrinsic intersection conditions do not themselves yield a contradiction. The two omitted vertices 0 and 6 and their joint constraints must be included in a successful exclusion. It is not necessary to keep trying random five- or six-point instances of this same relaxation.

The earlier orientation-only checks found 12 satisfiable generic sign systems and two timeouts, without imposing the hexagonal metric; determinant-product log inequalities did not exclude the remaining two. Numerical full-coordinate and common-core searches have not yielded a certified full torus. All such numerical failures are inconclusive.
