# Finite radical reconstruction of Type 5 paper tori

This package gives a reduction of Type 5 realization at any prescribed flat torus metric to eight continuous real variables and at most 32 algebraic branches per fixed marking. It also constructs one exact square-torus example by radicals (not an approximate zero of a nonlinear system). Types 4 and 7 have a related 14-variable reconstruction. These reductions do not establish universality or nonuniversality.

## 1. Setup

Faces (oriented): `012 031 024 053 045 152 136 164 147 175 263 237 274 256 357 465`. The universal vertices A={1,2,5} form a face. Roots R={3,4} are nonadjacent. Leaves L={0,6,7} are mutually nonadjacent and adjacent to every member of A union R. Thus the graph is K3 joined to K2,3. All facts about the graph and the links used below are checked by `verify_structure.py`.

Fix a lattice and its integral edge-shift cocycle K (regarded in physical Euclidean coordinates). By changing lifts, gauge every edge shift from vertex 1 to zero. Set intrinsic p1=0 and extrinsic r1=0. Since 125 is a face, K25=0. Fix p2,p5,p3,p4, eight real variables. After a rigid motion set r2=(p2,0), r5=(p5,0). These are noncollinear with r1. The distances from either root to the three anchors determine its planar projection by two linear equations and its signed height by a square root: at most two choices, including tangencies.

The core A union R must span R3 in any embedded realization. Otherwise the embedded graph on it would be planar K5 minus the edge 34 with both roots outside the triangular face 125. This is impossible: in every planar embedding of K5 minus ab, the triangle on the other three vertices separates a from b. For example, the graph has 5 vertices, 9 edges and 6 triangular faces on the sphere, and the three faces at a and three at b exhaust them; the common triangle is their separating boundary. Both roots are outside the filled face by injectivity.

## 2. Leaf reconstruction and degeneracy

For a leaf j let u=p_j and v=r_j be unknown. Put s_i=p_i-K_ij for each of its five core neighbors i. The edge equations are |v-r_i|^2=|u-s_i|^2. Since s_1=r_1=0, they are equivalent to

    Q(v,u)=|v|^2-|u|^2=0,
    2 r_i . v - 2 s_i . u = |r_i|^2 - |s_i|^2 (i=2,3,4,5).

The four linear equations have rank at least 3. We prove rank 4 for every embedded solution, and then show that the quadratic on the resulting affine line is not identically zero.

**Geometric observation.** A leaf star cannot be planar: its center, the three universals and one root would give an embedded planar K5. Nor can it consist of two planar half-stars on opposite sides of a common line through the center. In the nonplanar case the two transitions between the planes must occur at distinct, opposite rays on their intersection line (injectivity). The corresponding neighbors cannot be adjacent, so they must be roots 3,4. The three universal neighbors are off the line; two are in the same half-plane. Those two, the center and roots form a planar K5 minus 34 with both roots outside the triangle on the center and the two universals (the roots are on the line through the center). This contradicts separation. If the star has antipodal neighbor rays, its spherical link, of length exactly 2pi by intrinsic flatness, splits into two paths of length pi each, necessarily great-circle semicircles; hence it is one of the forbidden configurations. The same argument excludes any universal vertex lying between roots 3,4: its remaining neighbors are two universals and three leaves. In a two-half-star at least one half-plane contains a universal and another neighbor; the pair is adjacent and both are adjacent to the roots, again giving planar K5 minus 34. A planar universal star also contains K5. Consequently the five-cycle in a leaf link has no pair of consecutive collinear edges. If there were such a pair, its central vertex would lie between the other two (injectivity), which must be the only nonadjacent pair among the core, roots 3,4; the central vertex is universal, a contradiction.

**Rank 4.** Suppose the rank is 3. Because the r_i span R3, there is a linear map L:R3->R2 with s_i=L r_i. Relative to the leaf put R_i=r_i-v, S_i=s_i-u=L R_i+b, b=Lv-u. The edge equalities yield q(R_i,1)=0 for the homogeneous quadric

    q(R,t)=|R|^2-|LR+bt|^2.

For each pair of successive neighbors in the link, the lengths of R_i-R_k and S_i-S_k agree: the shifts are consistent in the incident triangle. Hence q(R_i-R_k,0)=0 and the entire projective line of that link edge lies on q=0. Its five lines have distinct consecutive lines, by the observation above.

If b is nonzero, align one intrinsic coordinate with b and replace t by ell_1(R)+|b|t; the form becomes |R|^2-ell_2(R)^2-t^2, of rank at least 3. If b=0 and rank <=2, the nonzero homogeneous form in R has zero locus contained in a plane or the union of two planes through the center (lower-dimensional loci cannot contain a nondegenerate triangle). Every incident face is in this zero locus, since its opposite link edge is in it and the form is homogeneous in R. If two different planes are used, the two transition edges have antipodal rays, giving the forbidden configuration. Rank 3 is impossible: in the nondegenerate three-dimensional quotient a totally isotropic subspace has dimension at most one, so every projective line on the quadric passes through its unique singular point. At each regular vertex there cannot be two distinct incident lines. Rank 4 with real lines requires signature (2,2); the projective quadric is equivalent to the rank-one 2x2 matrix quadric. Its lines have two rulings (fixed left factor or fixed right factor); distinct intersecting lines belong to opposite rulings. The link's odd cycle of five distinct consecutive lines cannot alternate rulings. All possibilities contradict embeddedness. Thus the rank is 4.

**Nonzero quadratic.** If Q vanishes identically along the affine solution line containing an actual embedded solution, a nonzero direction (e,d) satisfies |e|=|d| and e.(r_i-v)=d.(s_i-u) for every neighbor i (by the linear equations and derivative of Q). For each incident face the intrinsic-to-extrinsic linear isometry maps d to a vector of the same norm in the face plane. The two displayed inner-product identities identify it with the orthogonal projection of e onto that plane. Equal norms imply e is in the plane. Thus all face planes contain the common direction e, and any transitions between distinct planes must use edges parallel to e. By injectivity at most two such rays are possible, and if the star is not planar there must be two transitions, giving antipodal neighbor rays. The observation excludes both the planar and antipodal cases. Therefore Q is not identically zero. Each leaf has at most two admissible solutions. Since the leaves are mutually nonadjacent their reconstructions are independent; at most 2^2 * 2^3 = 32 branches for each core choice. All remaining face orientation, intrinsic covering and embedding tests must still be imposed. No generic-position assumption is used in the reduction.

## 3. Exact algebraic square example

Run `python verify.py` using Python 3's standard library. The intrinsic core coordinates are rational decimals explicitly given in the code, obtained from an earlier numerical square witness and then used as exact rational inputs. Roots are given by square roots of their squared heights. Leaves are given by solving the linear equations above followed by the quadratic formula. Thus the edge equations hold by construction: no numerical root residual is used as proof. Outward-rounded integer intervals with denominator 2^200 verify nonzero denominators, positive radicands, all 16 consistent nonzero intrinsic triangle orientations, degree of absolute value one, strict shortest-edge constraints, all 70 nonzero affine determinants and all 138 disjoint edge-face exclusions. The verifier also checks the shift cocycle sums around each face vanish. Signed double area equals twice the degree times lattice covolume, which is 1 here. All triangles are consistently oriented and nondegenerate, so a regular value has exactly one preimage. The PL map is locally open also at edges (opposite sides) and vertices (the cyclic star winds positively at least once). Hence absolute degree one implies the map is a homeomorphism to the square flat torus. In R3 all four-point determinants are nonzero; any unintended intersection of simplices would give a minimal affine dependence of at most five points with opposite signs in the two simplices. Such a dependence must have support five in general position and would entail a disjoint edge-triangle intersection, impossible by the checks. The facewise congruences consequently give an embedded isometric realization. `verification.txt` is the recorded output. This is an exact algebraic witness, although the reported decimal coordinate display is only for convenience.

## 4. Types 4 and 7: elimination of missing distances

For each graph use the five K4 anchors listed in `verify_structure.py`. Each outside vertex is adjacent to at least three anchor vertices. The anchor family covers all eight vertices and is connected by overlaps of three vertices. Such triples are noncollinear in an embedding since all three edges are present. If all five anchors were coplanar, the overlapping triples would force all eight vertices into one plane, impossible for an embedded closed torus. Thus some anchor is a nondegenerate tetrahedron. Fixing a marking and all fourteen intrinsic coordinates fixes its six edge lengths, hence its realization up to a Euclidean motion. Each outside vertex has at most two sphere-intersection choices from three known noncollinear anchor neighbors. Verify all remaining edges and embedding (for degeneracies use exact simplex intersection, not a strict general-position test). This gives at most 80 reconstruction branches, in fourteen continuous variables, without four extra unknown missing distances. The combinatorial checker verifies the anchor hypotheses, not the remaining inequalities.

## 5. Limitations

This work is not an independent audit of the prior Type 2 proof or earlier universality certificates. It does not close the Type 4 or Type 7 sharp-rhombic rays or Type 5 universality. Numerical searches including 300,000 Type 5 hexagonal core samples yielded no embedded witness; they were not exhaustive and give no nonexistence certificate. The square witness is close to a shortest-edge boundary. Continuation from it did not certify additional moduli. The general reconstruction arguments above need independent mathematical review. No novelty claim is made for the algebraic witness or the reduction without a literature audit.
