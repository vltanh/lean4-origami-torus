## Exact lower-degree hexagonal systems

The four symmetry representatives from the earlier winding reduction are encoded in `generate.py` using 29 real variables rather than a high-degree distance Gram matrix. The first face is fixed in the extrinsic xy-plane by a rigid motion. Intrinsic coordinates are scaled so that the lattice is generated by (1,0) and (1/2,1/2), with squared norm X^2+3Y^2 (and X^2+3Y^2+Z^2 in 3D). Consequently all coefficients are rational. The 21 unfixed edge length equations are quadratic. The face area constraints are quadratic; all affine minors have degree at most three.

The intrinsic PL map has consistently oriented nondegenerate triangles and degree one (sum of double signed areas is +/-1). A locally orientation-preserving triangulated map of this torus, with degree one, is a homeomorphism: around an edge there is a local homeomorphism and at a vertex the winding is a positive integer; the global degree one excludes multiple sheets or branching. The edge shifts are the already classified cocycles. Shortest-image inequalities are strict because an embedded straight edge cannot have a second shortest intrinsic path. A further necessary inequality is |r_i-r_j| <= d_T(p_i,p_j), which implies the squared diameter bound 1/3 by the hexagonal lattice covering radius. For nonedges the code adds inequalities for 25 lattice translates, all necessary (not intended to enumerate every translate).

For embeddedness, test disjoint simplices for intersection. Any prohibited intersection of simplices, even if they initially share vertices, produces a sign-compatible affine dependence after canceling the common coefficients. The positive and negative supports are disjoint subfaces. A minimal dependence can be chosen conformal to those signs, for example by taking an extreme ray of the cone of nonnegative coefficient solutions with the chosen signs. Its support has size at most five in R^3. For a support S of n points, the augmented affine matrix has rank n-1 and the kernel coefficients are nonzero. The rank test is vanishing of all affine minors with n-1 coordinate axes (automatic for n=5). Some choice of n-2 coordinate axes produces a full-row-rank augmented submatrix; the alternating cofactors of that submatrix give the kernel. The code forbids every all-strict sign-compatible cofactor vector, testing all such choices of axes. Thus coplanar and collinear cases are not silently dropped.

## A rigorous obstruction for a restricted Type 7 construction

Consider the twisted 4-by-2 periodic grid. Label the bottom and top rings B_i and T_i, i modulo 4. Its sixteen face sets are

- B_i B_{i+1} T_i, T_i T_{i+1} B_{i+1}
- B_i B_{i+1} T_{i+2}, T_i T_{i+1} B_{i+3}.

With labels B_0,...,B_3,T_0,...,T_3 = 0,...,7 the permutation (0,2,4,6,3,5,7,1) maps these face sets to Lutz Type 7.

**Proposition.** For the sharp-rhombic flat torus with lattice (1,0), (1/2,Y), Y >= sqrt(3)/2, this grid marking cannot give an embedded realization with the intrinsic rings at y=0 and y=Y/2 and their extrinsic images in two distinct parallel planes. Ring spacings and shapes may be arbitrary. This does not exclude other markings, unequal intrinsic level spacing, or nonplanar rings.

Proof. Let h be the distance between the extrinsic planes. Project to their common two-dimensional coordinate plane. Every triangular face has a ring edge as base and intrinsic altitude Y/2. The projection preserves the base and the component of the apex displacement along that edge; the squared projected altitude is therefore s^2=Y^2/4-h^2. A negative value is impossible. If s=0, the two triangles incident to each ring edge lie in the same plane and on the same side of that edge, contradicting embeddedness. If s>0, the projected apices must be on opposite sides of each edge: equal signed altitudes and equal extrinsic heights would again imply two triangles in the same plane on the same side of the edge. Thus the following midpoint incidences hold for projected vertices:

(T_i+T_{i+2})/2 lies on the line B_i B_{i+1}; (B_{i+1}+B_{i+3})/2 lies on the line T_i T_{i+1}.

Write the B ring as o+b+u, o-b+v, o+b-u, o-b-v, and the T ring as o+C+t+w, o+C-t+z, o+C+t-w, o+C-t-z. Here b,t are the half-differences of the two opposite-edge diagonal midpoints, C is centroid displacement, and x cross y denotes the planar determinant. Summing the midpoint equations at opposite edges gives u cross v = 2 b cross t, w cross z = 2 b cross t, b cross C = t cross C = 0. The B ring is a simple planar quadrilateral, so its nonzero signed area implies u cross v != 0. Hence b,t are independent and C=0.

Use affine coordinates with o=0, b=(1,0), t=(0,1). Subtracting opposite midpoint equations now gives v=Ju, z=Jw, where J(x,y)=(-y,x) in these affine coordinates (not a physical rotation). Also |u|^2=|w|^2=2 in these coordinates. Write u=(a,h0), w=(c,d), K=-a(c+d)+h0(c-d), M=a(c-d)+h0(c+d). Then K^2+M^2=8.

Let G be the positive definite physical Euclidean Gram matrix in the affine coordinates, and H=s^2 G/det G=[[A,D],[D,L]]. For each ring edge e and chosen incident projected apex p, squared altitude s^2 gives e^T H e = cross(e,p-base)^2. For B edges the cross products in cyclic order are K-2d, K+2c, K+2d, K-2c; for T they are K-2h0, K+2a, K+2h0, K-2a. Subtract opposite squared-altitude equations and divide by eight. The four equations are

```
 A(a+h0)-D(a-h0)+Kd = 0
-A(a-h0)-D(a+h0)-Kc = 0
 D(c+d)-L(c-d)+Kh0 = 0
-D(c-d)-L(c+d)-Ka = 0
```

Since a^2+h0^2=c^2+d^2=2, these imply A=L=K^2/4, D=-KM/4. Half the sum of the four B equations before subtraction gives 12A+4L-2K^2-8=0. Hence K^2=4, M^2=4 and det H=0, contradicting positive definiteness. QED.

The companion symbolic check independently matches the face lists and checks the algebraic identities by exact polynomial reduction. This proposition eliminates a whole proposed construction family, not Type 7 itself.

## Inconclusive experiments (not certificates)

- Vertical lift feasibility for a fixed generic planar projection can be reduced to finitely many strict linear height inequalities, one sign choice per overlapping connected set of faces. Floating LP experiments on the known rational planar families, including 1,266 eligible hexagonal Type 6 candidates and further samples away from the boundary, produced no new Type 4/5 embedded lift. These are not exhaustive over planar coordinates.
- Numerical diagonal-flip seam searches and symmetric Type 7 grid searches did not yield strictly feasible boundary witnesses.
- Direct fixed-metric constrained optimization (60 starts in each of the four orbits) found no positive separation margin at acceptable equality residuals. Zero or near-zero margins and solver failures do not exclude actual embeddings.
- Unequal-spacing parallel-ring trials also failed to find a strictly feasible witness; the floating circuit-sign test used in these trials is a sufficient test only, not complete for degenerate configurations. No impossibility theorem for unequal spacing is claimed.
